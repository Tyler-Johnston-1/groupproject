Ride share only vs. neither:

Participants who used ride share but not public transportation reported communication apprehension scores that were, on average, 0.94 points lower than participants who used neither mode, b = −0.94, SE = 0.27, t(248) = −3.44, p < .001.

Public transport only vs. neither:

Participants who used public transportation but not ride share reported communication apprehension scores that were, on average, 0.52 points lower than participants who used neither mode, b = −0.52, SE = 0.21, t(248) = −2.46, p = .015.

Both vs. neither:

Participants who used both ride share and public transportation reported communication apprehension scores that were, on average, 0.88 points lower than participants who used neither mode, b = −0.88, SE = 0.16, t(248) = −5.33, p < .001.

Overall model is significant:

Transportation-use group was significantly associated with communication apprehension, F(3, 248) = 10.38, p < .001, Rsqu = .112, so ~11.2% of the variance in communication apprehension is accounted for by which transportation group one falls into.

There was a significant negative linear trend in communication apprehension across increasing categories of ride-share frequency, after controlling for public-transport frequency, b = −0.64, SE = 0.19, t(243) = −3.47, p < .001.

i.e. Ps tended to have lower communication apprehension as ride-share use increased, even after accounting for differences in public transportation use.

AND

There was no evidence of a linear trend in communication apprehension across increasing categories of public-transport frequency after controlling for ride-share frequency, b = −0.13, SE = 0.15, t(243) = −0.86, p = .390.

i.e. After accounting for ride-share frequency, public transportation frequency was not significantly associated with communication apprehension.

exclusive_plot <- data_all |>
  filter(
    transport_group %in% c(
      "Ride share only",
      "Public transport only"
    )
  ) |>
  mutate(
    frequency = if_else(
      transport_group == "Ride share only",
      as.numeric(as.character(ride_share_monthly)),
      as.numeric(as.character(public_transport_monthly))
    )
  )

ggplot(
  exclusive_plot,
  aes(
    x = frequency,
    y = communication_apprehension,
    color = transport_group
  )
) +
  geom_jitter(width = 0.1, height = 0, alpha = 0.6) +
  geom_smooth(method = "lm", se = FALSE) +
  labs(
    x = "Monthly use frequency",
    y = "Communication apprehension",
    color = "Transportation mode"
  ) +
  theme_minimal()
`geom_smooth()` using formula = 'y ~ x'
